[Torven's NPC stats spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1dH5wkwZFACedf8gWVIBaozToU9lxxUDOVCHK_YXY2XU/edit?gid=982462317#gid=982462317) -
This is the largest compiled list of accurate NPC statistics there is outside of Darkpaw.  The stats in here are not guessed 
or made-up.  They were parsed or computed from carefully prepared logs using custom 
[scripts](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/0B9w2lZ-BZM3lMmxHMW9EQmsyMGs?resourcekey=0-eD4X4aE_vMYwW0b8Qpfnzw&usp=drive_link) 
and are either precise or have relatively small margins of error depending on the stat.  It was about eight years of labor
spanning from 2013 to 2021.

[Torven's AC & ATK spreadsheet](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B9w2lZ-BZM3lTDd1Z1dQelJ4MXM/view?usp=drive_link&resourcekey=0-tFuORnzoAe2_q7x_TgfK9w) - 
This has the results of parsing hundreds of carefully prepared logs on Live servers to compute estimates for NPC Mitigation AC,
Avoidance AC, Offense ATK and To-Hit.  It also has Arena dummy parses from 2015 which were done to figure out the melee
logic.  This data is in a more raw form and is not really intended for presentation.  Some of the results should have been
copied to the master NPC stats spreadsheet but I never got around to it.

[Torven's Pet Stats spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/10cwrKsjJhtY8fwgkevYaHCSmne7CX9Zu1xUHSgsZL9U/edit?gid=1123528453#gid=1123528453) - 
Torven parsed just about every summoned pet on Live servers in 2015 and this is the compiled results, along with some
older data.

The [ficticiousname9](https://web.archive.org/web/20010424070852/http://www.geocities.com/ficticiousname9/) Geocites
account was a well-known (at least by hardcore EQ players) late Kunark period list of NPCs collected by a 
Hackersquest forum member by the name of 
[EzSteve](https://web.archive.org/web/20010523082607/http://www.ethernalquest.com/ubb/Forum3/HTML/001199.html).
What makes this data particularly valuable is that it included maximum hitpoints.  Sony did not hide that from the 
client until the Velious launch timeframe.  It also includes NPC locations.  It's one of the best data sources of 
the old game ever compiled.

The Hackersquest folks also had an even earlier NPC list, which is the
[Zone-NPC-List-Level](https://web.archive.org/web/20001024142144/http://www.eqic.net/plain/files2.htm) data.  The file 
date is January 15 2000 and [this](https://web.archive.org/web/20000621024120/http://www.k2.org/main.htm) page also dates
it to Jan 15.  This has the original Paw NPCs in it, which dates some of the data to before December 10 1999.  This list 
includes NPC pets, so many classic era pet hitpoints can be determined from this.

[The Al'Kabor Project database](https://github.com/EQMacEmu/Server/tree/main/utils/sql/database_full)
is open source so the entire database can be downloaded from GitHub.  Emulated servers are generally not an acceptable 
source for this kind of research but as this is the most accurate public database and has curated data, I am including a 
link here.
